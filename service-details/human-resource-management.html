HR Management Services

Select a service to view the detailed process and requirements

Issuance of Service Record

Official record of employment history for LGU employees

Requirements

  • Duly accomplished request form
  • Valid government-issued ID
  • Authorization letter (if representative)
Step Activity Duration Personnel
1 Submit request form with requirements Ask the office HRMO Staff
2 Verification and preparation of service record Ask the office HRMO Staff
3 Review and signing by HRMO Head Ask the office HRMO Head
4 Release of Service Record Ask the office HRMO Staff

Processing Information

  • Total processing time: 27-42 minutes
  • Fee: Free for current employees

Issuance of Certificate of Employment

Official certification of current or previous employment status

Requirements

  • Request letter stating purpose
  • Valid government-issued ID
  • Employee ID (for current employees)
Step Activity Duration Personnel
1 Submit request letter and requirements Ask the office HRMO Staff
2 Verification of employment records Ask the office HRMO Staff
3 Preparation and signing of certificate Ask the office HRMO Head
4 Release of Certificate of Employment Ask the office HRMO Staff

Processing Information

  • Total processing time: 27 minutes
  • Fee: Free

Issuance of Certificate of Leave Credits

Official statement of accumulated leave credits (vacation and sick leave)

Requirements

  • Request form/letter
  • Employee ID
Step Activity Duration Personnel
1 Submit request for leave credits certificate Ask the office HRMO Staff
2 Computation and verification of leave balances Ask the office HRMO Staff
3 Preparation and signing of certificate Ask the office HRMO Head
4 Release of Certificate Ask the office HRMO Staff

Processing Information

  • Total processing time: 25 minutes
  • Fee: Free

Application for Leave

Processing of vacation leave, sick leave, and other leave types

Requirements

  • Duly accomplished CS Form No. 6 (Application for Leave)
  • Medical certificate (for sick leave exceeding 5 days)
  • Supporting documents (as applicable)
Step Activity Duration Personnel
1 Submit accomplished leave application form Ask the office HRMO Staff
2 Verification of leave credits and computation Ask the office HRMO Staff
3 Recommendation by Department Head Ask the office Department Head
4 Approval by Municipal Mayor Ask the office HRMO
5 Recording and release of approved leave Ask the office HRMO Staff
Leave Type Description
Vacation Leave For personal matters, rest, and recreation
Sick Leave For illness or medical reasons
Maternity Leave 105 days for female employees
Paternity Leave 7 days for male employees
Special Leave For special circumstances as provided by law
