Visit Sibale

Visit Sibale

Beaches, dive sites and trails, how to get here, visitor fees and where to stay

Check advisories before you travel

Boats do not sail in bad weather or under a gale warning, and classes or work may be suspended. The Public Information Office posts every suspension and weather update.

Destinations

Places to see

Sibale has about 15 dive sites, white-sand beaches and old trails. Dive only with certified operators, keep your distance from turtles, and take your rubbish home.

Bagulayag Cliff

Poblacion

A 50-metre sea cliff with a view over Banton Pass and the Tablas Strait.

Tonggo Beach

About half a kilometre of white sand where sea turtles nest; now a protected marine sanctuary.

Guiwanon Beach

A long white-sand beach with a wide reef.

Mount Batariya

A 100-metre peak at the mouth of Concepcion Bay, topped by an image of the Immaculate Conception.

Gran Tsina Ridge

A high plateau named after Chinese traders; Ming-dynasty porcelain was found here in the 1970s.

Mactan Shipwreck

off Masudsud

Wreck of the M/V Mactan, lost in 1973; a protected dive site.

Coral Cathedral

A coral pinnacle dive site.

Tinigban Trail

Poblacion

A Spanish-era carved rock beside a former waterfall.

Quebrada Beach

White sand facing Mount Batariya across Concepcion Bay.

Guiob Beach

A quiet beach reached by motorcycle from Poblacion.

Maisuting Baybay Beach

A white beach inside a fish sanctuary; only handline fishing is allowed.

Cambilog Beach

A long, quiet beach with rocky points.

Visitor fee

Carbon Footprint Mitigation Fee

Paid once per visit when you arrive, at the port or a collection point. Keep your ticket: the Treasurer's Office runs inspections, and not paying is fined ₱300. Set by Municipal Ordinance No. 2024-12.

Getting here

From Metro Manila to Sibale

There is no airport. Leave Manila around 10:00 PM and you arrive the next afternoon. Expect about ₱1,715–1,785 one way, not counting meals. Students, seniors and persons with disabilities get 20% off fares.

  • There is no bank or ATM on the island. Bring enough cash.
  • There is no public transport on the island: hire a habal-habal (motorcycle taxi) or a small boat.
  • Mobile signal varies around the island.

Feasts

Feasts and festivals

  • December 8 · Town fiesta, feast of the Immaculate Conception, with the Rungawan Festival, the Kami Ray Barangay Festival and the Bangkarera boat race in the days before.
  • April · Barangay feasts and homecomings: St. Vincent Ferrer in San Vicente, St. Mark in Poblacion, Balik Dalajican and Balik Sampong.
