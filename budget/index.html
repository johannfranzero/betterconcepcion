Records

Where the Official Records Are

These are the official places to find Concepcion's financial documents.

DILG Full Disclosure Policy Portal

Annual budget, statement of receipts and expenditures, procurement and trust fund reports that LGUs must post. No Concepcion postings found yet.

COA Annual Audit Reports

The Commission on Audit's yearly audit of the municipality. Concepcion, Romblon's reports are still being located.

Bureau of Local Government Finance

Statements of receipts and expenditures (SRE) and income classification data for every LGU.

DBM: National Tax Allotment

Each LGU's share of national taxes (NTA) is published in DBM's annual local budget memorandum.

What we checked (25 Nov 2026). The FY 2027 national budget proposal lists public works for Romblon only as totals for the whole Romblon District Engineering Office, not by town or barangay (BetterGov Budget search). Barangay shares of the National Tax Allotment are issued by DBM in its local budget memorandums; the Concepcion figures have not been extracted yet.

Where the barangay budget is. Every barangay must post its approved annual budget on its Full Disclosure Policy board at the barangay hall, and anyone can request a copy. If you have a photo or copy, please share it on GitHub so it can be added here with its source.

Infrastructure

Projects in Concepcion

Projects and their status as reported by the municipality's Public Information Office. Amounts are shown only where the LGU published them.

Circumferential road, San Pedro to Calabasahan

₱25,000,000

Widening and upgrading of about 900 metres of road with 1.5-metre shoulders, serving 331 households (1,125 residents). Funded by the FY 2026 Local Government Support Fund – Growth Equity Fund (LGSF-GEF).

Under construction (site inspection, August 2026)

Gaylang water system, Calabasahan

Amount not published

A Small Water Impounding Project (SWIP) dam at Gaylang feeds a new water filtration facility that pipes treated water to homes. Water quality was tested at the facility before a full system test.

Testing (July 2026)

Masursor Arch Bridge, Sapangpalay, Masudsud

No public funds

A self-supporting timber arch based on a Leonardo da Vinci design, built without nails or permanent fasteners. Most of the timber is donated or reused ROMELCO power poles.

Opened (August 2026)

Covered courts in Masadya, Dalajican, San Pedro and Calabasahan

Amount not published

Inspected by Representative Eleandro Jesus Madrona and Mayor Fameronag together with barangay officials.

Inspected (August 4, 2026)

Pabahay housing project

Amount not published

Disaster-resilient homes for families relocated from high-risk coastal and watershed areas. The first three families received certificates of award.

First units turned over (November 9, 2026)

Proposed RoPax ferry

Not yet funded

A modern steel-hulled roll-on/roll-off passenger ferry for Concepcion, discussed with ferry operators and investment groups.

Proposal (October 22, 2026)
