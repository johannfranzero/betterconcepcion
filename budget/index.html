Where the Official Records Are

These are the official places to find Concepcion's financial documents.

DILG Full Disclosure Policy Portal

Annual budget, statement of receipts and expenditures, procurement and trust fund reports that LGUs must post. No Concepcion postings found yet.

COA Annual Audit Reports

The Commission on Audit's yearly audit of the municipality. Concepcion, Romblon's reports are still being located.

Bureau of Local Government Finance

Statements of receipts and expenditures (SRE) and income classification data for every LGU.

DBM: National Tax Allotment

Each LGU's share of national taxes (NTA) is published in DBM's annual local budget memorandum.
