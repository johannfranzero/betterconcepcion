Getting There from Manila

Bus: Buendia or PITX to Batangas Pier ~10:00 PM · 2–2.5 hrs · ₱280–310
RoRo: Batangas to Calapan ~1:00 AM · 2–2.5 hrs · ₱680–720 + ₱90 terminal fee
Van or mini bus: Calapan to Pinamalayan 1.5–2.5 hrs · ₱200
Tricycle to Recodo Port ₱25 · arrive before 9:00 AM
Motorboat: Recodo Port to Sibale 10:00 AM · 2.5–3 hrs · ₱390
Good to know Falcon Dos and Arcem Liner alternate days; trips are cancelled in bad weather. There is no airport on Sibale. About ₱1,715–1,785 one way, excluding meals.
Visitor fee Carbon Footprint Mitigation Fee: ₱50 for local non-resident tourists, ₱100 foreign, ₱40 students, PWDs and seniors, free under 14.

Source: visitsibale.com travel guide. Schedules and fares change; confirm with the Tourism Office before travelling.
