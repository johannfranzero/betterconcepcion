### Getting there
- Most trips go by boat from **Pinamalayan Port, Oriental Mindoro**, a crossing of about 2 to 3 hours. An older Romblon News guide gives one trip a day at 11:00 AM for PHP 150, but this schedule and fare are UNVERIFIED and may be outdated. The guide also says there are no ATMs on the island. Source: https://archive.romblonnews.net/index.php/tourism-360/3049-travel-guide-trip-to-bagulayag-of-sibale-island
- Wikipedia puts Pinamalayan about 2 hours away by boat and Romblon town about 5 hours away, and says many residents trade in Pinamalayan rather than in Odiongan or Romblon. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Concepcion,_Romblon
- As of Sept 2022 the town relied on three wooden-hulled boats on the Concepcion–Pinamalayan route. MARINA had ordered them phased out, and Mayor Fameronag had asked PACIWFI for a steel-hulled RoRo passenger ship, which was expected in 2023. Whether that ship now operates is UNVERIFIED. Sources: https://tribune.net.ph/2022/09/07/modernizing-concepcion-romblons-sea-transport ; https://tribune.net.ph/2022/12/14/mini-roro-passenger-ship-for-concepcion-romblon

### Languages
- Most residents speak **Asi (Bantoanon)**, which is also spoken on Banton and Simara. Tagalog is also used. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Concepcion,_Romblon

### History summary
Under Spanish rule Concepcion was a village administered as part of Banton and was named after the Immaculate Conception. Wikipedia dates its founding to 1570 (UNVERIFIED). It became a municipality on 2 July 1907. It was later joined to Mindoro and then returned to Romblon when the province was restored in 1917. Commonwealth Act No. 581 (1940) made it a special municipality. Republic Act No. 38 undid this, and municipal status was restored around 1 October 1946. Residents have periodically pushed to rejoin Oriental Mindoro: a 1924 bill (Act No. 3131) failed, and in 2014 a former mayor said most residents favoured annexation. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Concepcion,_Romblon

### Fiesta / patron saint
- Patron: **Immaculate Conception** (Church of the Immaculate Conception, Diocese of Romblon). Sources: https://gcatholic.org/churches/asia-east/64309 ; https://gcatholic.org/churches/local/romb0.htm
- Feast day: December 8 is the liturgical feast of the Immaculate Conception. A **municipal fiesta on that date is UNVERIFIED**, since no source confirmed the town's fiesta date.

### Tourism spots (all UNVERIFIED, Wikipedia only)
Mount Banderahan (a summit with a stone-set flagpole), Tinigban Falls (with ruins of an old Spanish-era mansion), Matudtod Cave (where ancient remains were found), Quebrada Beach, Tongo Point, and the beaches between Bachawan and Sampong. Diving is also offered. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Concepcion,_Romblon
- Daily Tribune says each of the 9 barangays has a cove and a dive spot, and that a tourism site counts at least 19 dive sites around Sibale. Source: https://tribune.net.ph/2022/09/07/modernizing-concepcion-romblons-sea-transport
