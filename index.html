Brief History of Concepcion

1570

Spanish expedition of Goiti and Salcedo lands; the island is named Maestre de Campo.

1622

Named Concepcion after the Immaculate Conception and administered under Banton.

1907

Became a municipality on 2 July 1907, then part of Mindoro.

1917

Returned to Romblon when the province was restored, after a period under Mindoro.

1940

Reverted to a barrio under Commonwealth Act No. 581.

1946

Regained municipal status under Republic Act No. 38.

An Island Town

Concepcion covers all of Maestro de Campo Island, which residents call Sibale. It lies about 17 km from Pinamalayan, Oriental Mindoro, and is reached by boat in about 2–3 hours.

Language

Most residents speak Asi (Bantoanon), also spoken on Banton and Simara. Tagalog and English are understood. The town fiesta is on 8 December, feast of the Immaculate Conception.
