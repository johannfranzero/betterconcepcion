Brief History of Concepcion

Spanish era

Concepcion began as a village administered as part of Banton and was named after the Immaculate Conception.

1907

Became a municipality on 2 July 1907.

1917

Returned to Romblon when the province was restored, after a period under Mindoro.

1940

Made a special municipality under Commonwealth Act No. 581.

1946

Regular municipal status restored under Republic Act No. 38.

An Island Town

Concepcion covers all of Maestro de Campo Island, which residents call Sibale. It lies about 17 km from Pinamalayan, Oriental Mindoro, and is reached by boat in about 2–3 hours.

Language

Most residents speak Asi (Bantoanon), also spoken on Banton and Simara. Tagalog is widely used.
